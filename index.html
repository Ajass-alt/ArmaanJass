About

If I could put into words the feeling I get when I solve a problem I couldn't at first, I probably wouldn't be an engineer. It's right up there with bombing a drive down the fairway or scoring a goal in front of the people you love.

My recent work centres on designing, simulating, and fabricating parts for drones and cars, alongside hands-on experience as an irrigation technician working directly with mechanical, electrical, and hydraulic systems outside of a CAD screen.

My interest in engineering traces back to watching Iron Man for the first time. Since then I've chased the same loop: learn something, apply it, then go learn more.
